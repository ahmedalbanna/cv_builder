## 🎯 First-Time User Flow

1. **Personal Information** (Required)
   - Name, email, phone, location
   - Professional summary
   - Social links (optional)

2. **Work Experience** (Click "Add Work Experience")
   - Company, position, dates
   - Job description
   - Add multiple entries

3. **Education** (Click "Add Education")
   - Institution, degree, field
   - Dates and GPA (optional)

4. **Skills** (Add your expertise)
   - Choose category (Technical, Soft Skills, etc.)
   - Select proficiency level
   - Add as many as you need

5. **Optional Sections**
   - Projects (with technologies)
   - Certifications (with credentials)
   - Languages (with proficiency)

6. **Choose Template**
   - Modern (colorful, tech-friendly)
   - Classic (formal, traditional)
   - Creative (two-column, designer-friendly)

7. **Export**
   - Click "Export to PDF"
   - Your CV downloads automatically

## 💾 Data Storage

Your CV data is stored locally in your browser:
- ✅ **Auto-saved** on every change
- ✅ **Private** - never sent to any server
- ✅ **Persistent** - saved across sessions
- ⚠️ **Local only** - not synced across devices
- ⚠️ **Browser-specific** - clearing data deletes CV

**Pro Tip**: Export to PDF regularly to back up your work!

## 🆘 Need Help?

### Common Questions

**Q: Where is my data stored?**
A: In your browser's local storage. It never leaves your computer.

**Q: Can I edit my CV later?**
A: Yes! Your data is auto-saved. Just return to the app and continue editing.

**Q: How do I switch templates?**
A: Click on any template in the template selector. Preview updates instantly.

**Q: What if I want to start over?**
A: Clear your browser's local storage or fill in new data to overwrite.

**Q: Does this work offline?**
A: The app works offline after first load, but you need internet for Google Fonts.

## 🎯 Pro Tips

1. **Fill sections gradually** - You don't need to complete everything at once
2. **Use the preview** - Check how your CV looks while editing
3. **Try all templates** - Switch between them to find your favorite
4. **Keep it concise** - Employers spend ~6 seconds on first scan
5. **Export regularly** - Download your CV as a backup
6. **Use keywords** - Include industry-specific terms for ATS systems
7. **Update often** - Keep your CV current with recent achievements
