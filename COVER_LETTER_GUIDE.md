# Cover Letter Builder Guide

## Overview
The CV Builder application now includes a comprehensive **Cover Letter Builder** feature that allows you to create professional cover letters alongside your resume.

## Features

### ✨ Key Capabilities
- **3 Professional Templates**: Professional, Modern, and Simple designs
- **Smart Form Fields**: Organized sections for sender, recipient, company, and letter content
- **Import from CV**: One-click import of your contact information from your CV
- **Real-time Preview**: See your cover letter update as you type
- **PDF Export**: Download your cover letter as a high-quality PDF
- **Auto-save**: All data is automatically saved to local storage
- **Multiple Profiles**: Create and manage multiple cover letters
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on desktop, tablet, and mobile

---

## How to Use

### 1. Access the Cover Letter Builder
- Click the **"Cover Letter"** tab in the header navigation
- The interface will switch from CV mode to Cover Letter mode

### 2. Fill in Your Information

#### **Your Information (Sender)**
- Full name, email, phone
- Complete address with city, state, ZIP, and country
- Click **"Import from CV"** to auto-fill from your resume

#### **Recipient Information**
- Hiring manager name and title
- Company name and department
- Complete mailing address

#### **Position & Company Details**
- Date of the letter
- Job title/position you're applying for
- Job reference number (if applicable)
- Company name and industry
- How you found the position

#### **Letter Content**
- **Opening Paragraph**: Introduce yourself and state the position
- **Body Paragraph 1**: Highlight relevant experience and skills
- **Body Paragraph 2**: Explain your interest in the company
- **Body Paragraph 3** (Optional): Additional achievements or qualifications
- **Closing Paragraph**: Thank them and request an interview
- **Salutation**: Choose from professional closings (Sincerely, Best regards, etc.)

### 3. Choose a Template
Select from three professional templates:
- **Professional**: Traditional business letter format
- **Modern**: Contemporary design with colored header
- **Simple**: Clean, minimalist layout

### 4. Export Your Cover Letter
- Click the **"Export PDF"** button
- Your cover letter will be downloaded as a PDF file
- Filename format: `[YourName]_[CompanyName].pdf`

## Tips for Writing Great Cover Letters

### Opening Paragraph
- Mention the specific position you're applying for
- State how you found the job (referral, job board, company website)
- Express genuine enthusiasm for the opportunity
- Keep it concise (3-4 sentences)

### Body Paragraphs
- **Paragraph 1**: Focus on your most relevant experience
  - Use specific examples and achievements
  - Quantify results when possible
  - Match your skills to job requirements

- **Paragraph 2**: Show company knowledge
  - Demonstrate you've researched the company
  - Explain why you want to work there
  - Align your values with the company's mission

- **Paragraph 3** (Optional): Additional selling points
  - Unique qualifications or experiences
  - Relevant projects or accomplishments
  - Career transition explanations

### Closing Paragraph
- Thank them for their consideration
- Express eagerness for an interview
- Provide availability for follow-up
- Include your contact information

## Keyboard Shortcuts & Tips

### Best Practices
- **Save time**: Use "Import from CV" to avoid retyping contact info
- **Customize**: Tailor each cover letter to the specific job and company
- **Proofread**: Always review before exporting to PDF
- **Be specific**: Mention the company name and position throughout
- **Keep it concise**: Aim for one page (3-5 paragraphs)

### Professional Writing Tips
- Use active voice and strong action verbs
- Avoid generic phrases ("I am writing to apply...")
- Show, don't tell (provide examples of your skills)
- Match your tone to the company culture
- Proofread for spelling and grammar errors

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Cover letter not saving?**
- Check that local storage is enabled in your browser
- Clear browser cache and reload the page

**PDF export not working?**
- Ensure you've filled in required fields (marked with *)
- Try using a different browser
- Check that pop-ups are not blocked

**Template not displaying correctly?**
- Refresh the page
- Clear browser cache
- Try switching templates and back

**Import from CV not working?**
- Make sure you've created and saved a CV first
- Switch to the CV tab and verify your personal information is filled in
- Switch back to Cover Letter and try importing again
